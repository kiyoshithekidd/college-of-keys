Trainer's Guild Orientation

How to Cast Musical Spells in the College of Keys
🎹
1. The 61-Key Arcane Keyboard (C2–C7)

Connect any physical 61-key USB MIDI keyboard for native zero-latency response. Alternatively, play using your computer's A S D F G H J K L ; QWERTY keys (with Z / X to shift octaves) or mouse clicks.

✨
2. The Spell Highway

Glyphs fall toward the golden Strike Line. Radiant Gold is your Right Hand (Lead); Eldritch Blue is your Left Hand (Accompaniment). Staggered chords roll cleanly.

⏳
3. Wait Mode & 100ms Chord Roll

In Wait Mode, playback freezes at the strike line until you play the required pitches. Rolling a chord within 100ms registers as a clean strike!

🔥
4. Bardic Inspiration & XP

Build a 50+ Spell Chain to trigger Bardic Inspiration for 2x XP and an arcane tavern glow. At song end, roll your d20 Performance Check!
